Does Avis have a real promo code?

Yes. Avis’s official coupon page lists UUWA036 for a free vehicle upgrade and several AWD numbers for published discounts.

When do I enter an Avis code?

Avis says to enter coupon or AWD codes at the beginning of the reservation, before selecting your car.

Can I combine codes?

Avis says AWD numbers can be stacked with coupon codes, but multiple AWD numbers or multiple coupon codes cannot be combined.
