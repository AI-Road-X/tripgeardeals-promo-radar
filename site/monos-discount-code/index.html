How to get current official Monos savings

  1. Open Monos's official U.S. Sale page and choose an available discounted item if it fits the purchase.
  2. Add the item to cart and confirm the price and stock status shown for the selected variant.
  3. For free shipping, confirm that the order exceeds $100 and ships within the contiguous United States or Canada using standard shipping.
  4. At checkout, check whether the order exceeds $150 and select an available free gift if the official checkout presents one. Do not enter a third-party code when Monos has not published it on its official pages.

Monos discount code FAQs

Does Monos publish a current discount or promo code?

The official Monos U.S. pages checked for this guide do not publicly list a current discount or promo code. This guide therefore does not list a code from third-party coupon sites.

How do I get Monos free shipping?

Monos says it offers free standard shipping on all orders over $100 within the contiguous United States and Canada. Other locations and expedited or express shipping are calculated at checkout.

What is the current Monos sale?

Monos's official Sale page says “Save 15% on select suitcases, bags, and travel favorites.” Check the official sale listing for the selected product's current price, availability, and any pre-order status.
