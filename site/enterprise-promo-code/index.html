How to use an official Enterprise rental offer

  1. Open Enterprise's official deals page and select the offer or booking link that matches your rental.
  2. Check the pickup location, rental length, vehicle class, dates, and any blackout or participation limits before booking.
  3. If Enterprise provides a code or eligibility link for your offer, enter it in the reservation flow before the rental is picked up; do not expect a discount to be added after pickup.
  4. Review the base rate, taxes, fees, exclusions, and final total before completing the reservation.

Enterprise Rent-A-Car promo code FAQs

Does Enterprise publish a current public promo code?

The official Enterprise pages checked do not display a current reusable public promo-code value. Current savings are presented through official deal links, membership benefits, partnership offers, or eligibility-based rates.

How does the current Enterprise rental deal work?

Enterprise currently lists up to 15% off the base rate for a rental of 3 days or more. The linked offer has its own terms and says it cannot be combined with another discount or applied to a previous or existing reservation or rental.

Is Enterprise Plus a promo code?

No. Enterprise Plus is a membership program that can provide points and free rental-day benefits under its terms. It is not a public promo code.

Can a discount be added after pickup?

Enterprise's official FAQ says a discount cannot be added after the customer has already picked up the vehicle. Check eligibility and apply the applicable offer before pickup.
