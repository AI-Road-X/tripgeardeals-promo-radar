How to use a WallyPark promo code

  1. Choose your airport, parking type, and reservation dates on WallyPark.com or in the WallyPark app.
  2. Before continuing, select Apply Promo and enter an eligible code.
  3. Review the updated promo rate and the offer terms before confirming the reservation.
  4. Bring any required membership or eligibility identification to checkout when using AAA, senior, military, or government discounts.

WallyPark promo code FAQs

Where do I enter a WallyPark promo code?

After selecting a parking type, choose Apply Promo instead of continuing, then enter the code. WallyPark says eligible current email offers may also appear for customers who opted in to marketing emails.

Can I combine WallyPark promo codes?

No. WallyPark’s terms say promotional codes cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. Its FAQ also says only one promotional code can be redeemed online when making a reservation.

Why is my WallyPark promo code not working?

Check the code’s reservation dates, stay length, location restrictions, and eligibility. WallyPark says promo codes cannot be used for drive-up stays, and eligibility-based discounts require supporting identification at checkout.
