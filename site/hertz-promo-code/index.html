How to use a current official Hertz offer

  1. Open the matching official Hertz offer page and start the reservation from that page so its promotion is attached to the search.
  2. For codes 212157 or 210751, confirm that the code appears in the reservation and that the rental length, vehicle class, participating location, and pickup date meet the official terms.
  3. For PC 211748, reserve an eligible medium sedan or higher and confirm the upgrade at the rental location because it remains subject to vehicle availability.
  4. For an AAA rate, use the official AAA page to retrieve the club CDP from the member's ZIP code, include it in the reservation, and bring the valid AAA membership card to the rental counter.
  5. Before payment, confirm the base-rate discount and all taxes, fees, options, exclusions, and pickup dates. Do not combine an offer with another discount or special rate when the terms prohibit it.

Hertz promo code FAQs

What official Hertz promo codes are currently listed?

Hertz currently lists 212157 for 10% off eligible EV and hybrid rentals, 210751 for one free day on eligible rentals of at least seven consecutive days, and 211748 for an eligible one-car-class upgrade. Each has its own rental, vehicle, location, and pickup-date restrictions.

Is the Hertz AAA discount a public coupon code?

No. It is an AAA membership benefit. Hertz instructs AAA members to enter their ZIP code to find the applicable AAA Club Discount Code, include it in the reservation, and present their AAA membership card at rental.

Can I combine a Hertz promotion code with another offer?

No for the offers listed in this guide. Their official terms say they cannot be combined with other discounts, promotions, offers, or special rates.

What does the Hertz Pay Now offer cover?

Hertz lists savings of up to 25% compared with the Pay Later base rate at participating U.S. and Canadian locations. The amount varies, taxes, fees, and options are excluded, Dream Collection vehicles are excluded, and a 24-hour advance reservation is required.
