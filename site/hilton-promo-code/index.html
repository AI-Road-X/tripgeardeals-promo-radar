How to use an official Hilton rate or offer

  1. Open Hilton's official offers page and choose the rate or offer that matches your membership or eligibility.
  2. Sign in to Hilton Honors when the selected member rate requires an account, or use the official corporate or AAA/CAA route when eligible.
  3. Check the selected hotel's dates, room type, advance-purchase or cancellation terms, and whether the rate can be combined with another offer.
  4. Complete the booking through Hilton.com, the Hilton Honors app, or the official channel stated by the offer, then review the final price and conditions.

Hilton promo code FAQs

Does Hilton have one universal public promo code?

No universal reusable Hilton code was verified on the official pages checked. Hilton's savings routes are tied to member, corporate, AAA/CAA, advance-purchase, or specific promotion eligibility.

Is the Hilton Honors discount a public promo code?

No. It is a member rate accessed through Hilton channels after signing in or joining Hilton Honors, subject to the official terms.

Who can use Hilton corporate discount rates?

Travelers with the applicable corporate eligibility can use the corporate discount route described by Hilton. The rate is not a general public code and its requirements must be met at booking.

Can Hilton Honors rates be combined with other discounts?

Hilton's terms say the Hilton Honors Discount rate cannot be combined with other promotions, offers, or discounts and is not valid for existing reservations or groups.
