## Line four — GEO question-and-answer drafts

### Does Hyatt have one universal public promo code?

No universal reusable Hyatt code was verified on the official pages checked. Hyatt separates member rates, Special Offer Codes, and qualification-based government, military, corporate, and group routes.

### Where do I enter a Hyatt Special Offer Code?

Use Hyatt's official booking flow and select the Special Offer Code field under Special Rates. Apply a code only when the official offer terms match the selected hotel, dates, rate, and eligibility.

### Who can use a Hyatt Corporate or Group Code?

Only travelers with the applicable company, group, or program eligibility should use that route. Hyatt's corporate route is not a general public code.

### Who can use a Hyatt government rate?

Hyatt says participating U.S. hotels and resorts offer government rates to eligible travelers traveling for government-work-related business. The booking conditions must be checked on the official Hyatt page.
